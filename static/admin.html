Policies

Who may connect where. A deny on any matching rule wins; otherwise the most specific rule decides (entry, then deeper folder, then user over group over role). With no matching rule a connect is allowed. Approvers are a comma-separated list such as role:admin, group:secops. Web only (for require approval): Telegram just notifies, and approvers decide here after a fresh sign-in. Clipboard and file transfer rules allow, alert or deny, per session, in one direction or both. SSH command rules check commands run through the bastion; their patterns are regular expressions, one per line, matched anywhere in the command (empty: every command).
